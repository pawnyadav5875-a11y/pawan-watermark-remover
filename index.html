How to Use / कैसे इस्तेमाल करें

  1. Choose a tool and select an image from your device.
  2. For background removal, press Remove background and wait for model processing.
  3. For Magic Eraser, paint over the object in red, then press Erase selected.
  4. For image generation, enter a prompt, choose a ratio and press Generate.
  5. Use the download button to save your result.

Large images may use significant device memory. Close other tabs if processing fails.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

The app has no paid backend. Third-party model/API availability, rate limits and policies can change.

Are my uploaded photos stored?

The Flask server does not receive images. Editing is performed locally in your browser. The generator sends your prompt to Pollinations and retrieves the generated image.

Why is background removal slow?

The browser may need to download neural-network model assets on first use. Device speed and available RAM affect processing.

Does the eraser always remove objects perfectly?

No. It uses local diffusion-based inpainting, not a large generative fill model. Results depend on the background and mask size.

Can I use it offline?

The app shell can load after it has been cached. AI model downloads and image generation require internet access.

About Pawan Studio AI

Pawan Studio AI is a lightweight creative web app designed to make everyday image editing accessible. It combines browser-side image processing with an optional third-party image-generation service.

It is an independent project and is not affiliated with IMG.LY or Pollinations.

Privacy Policy

Local image processing: Images selected for background removal and magic erasing are processed in your browser. This Flask application has no image-upload endpoint and does not store your source or edited images on its server.

Third-party services: Background-removal code/model assets are loaded from a CDN. When you use image generation, your prompt and generation request are sent to Pollinations; its own privacy practices apply. Your browser may also contact Render to load this app and its assets.

Advertising: If ads are enabled, ad providers may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. Update this policy with the exact providers and consent requirements before enabling ads.

Data retention: This app does not create user accounts or maintain an image library. Downloaded files remain under your control.
